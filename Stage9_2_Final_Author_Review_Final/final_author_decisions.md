## 1. Permutation Importance Discrepancy Resolution

* **Background:**  
  Independent review noted a minor discrepancy between two values reported in earlier draft summaries for the permutation importance of antecedent surface runoff (`sro_lag_1d`): **$+2.102\text{ mm}$** ($+37.6\%$) vs. **$+2.3545\text{ mm}$** ($+42.16\%$).
* **Forensic Source Audit:**
  - **Authoritative Source Artifact:** `Stage5_Model_Interpretation/permutation_importance.csv` generated by `stage5_task1_feature_shap.py`.
  - **Model:** LightGBM Tweedie (`m_lgb_tweedie`).
  - **Evaluation Partition:** Full 2023 Validation Partition ($N = 91,980$).
  - **Metric:** Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE).
  - **Repetition Count:** 3 random seeds (`seeds = [42, 101, 2024]`).
  - **Baseline Validation Score:** $\text{RMSE} \approx 5.5847\text{ mm}$.
  - **Authoritative Result:**
    - `sro_lag_1d`: Mean $\Delta \text{RMSE} = \mathbf{+2.3545\text{ mm}}$ (standard deviation $\sigma = 0.0220\text{ mm}$), representing a **$+42.16\%$** increase in validation RMSE.
    - `src_lag_1d`: Mean $\Delta \text{RMSE} = +2.1241\text{ mm}$ ($\sigma = 0.0424\text{ mm}$, $+38.03\%$).
    - `strd_lag_1d`: Mean $\Delta \text{RMSE} = +1.7960\text{ mm}$ ($\sigma = 0.0328\text{ mm}$, $+32.16\%$).
  - **Origin of $+2.102\text{ mm}$:** A transcription typo in Table 1 of `stage5_scientific_interpretation_report.md` that had been repeated in narrative prose.
* **Author Action / Recommendation:**  
  The manuscript narrative has been updated to cite the authoritative CSV value: **$\Delta \text{RMSE} = +2.35\text{ mm}$ ($+42.2\%$)**, while emphasizing the qualitative scientific finding that antecedent land-surface runoff is the single most sensitive empirical predictor.
